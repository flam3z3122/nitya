Privacy policy

Last updated September 9, 2026

Who we are

daymark is a habit, task, and goal tracker. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app and website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

Account details: the email address and password you use to sign in, plus the display name you choose. You may optionally verify your email later from Profile.

App data you create: habits, tasks, goals, check-ins, skips, reminder times, and progress.

Preferences: theme and whether reminders are enabled.

Technical data: a sign-in session so you stay logged in on this device. We do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, show your workspace, send optional in-app reminder notifications you turn on, and improve reliability of the service.

Reminders use your browser or device notification permission. They may not fire if the app is fully closed.

Where it is stored

Your account and workspace data are stored with our database provider, Supabase. Access is limited to your signed-in account through row-level security.

Your choices

You can edit your name, theme, and reminder setting in Profile.

You can delete habits, tasks, and goals from the app.

You can delete your entire account in Profile. That removes your sign-in and the workspace data tied to it.

Children

daymark is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page will change when we do.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, use the email address on your daymark account or contact the developer who published this app listing.
