Delete your daymark account

This page is for the daymark habit tracker on Google Play and the web.

How to delete your account

  1. Open todayshabit.netlify.app (or the daymark app) and sign in with the email you used to create the account.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete next to Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

If you cannot sign in, use the support email on the daymark Play Store listing and ask us to delete the account. Include the email address on the account.

What is deleted

When you delete your account we remove:

  • Your sign-in (email and password)
  • Your display name and profile settings
  • Habits, tasks, goals, check-ins, skips, reminder times, and progress
  • The session that keeps you signed in on this device

What we keep

We do not keep a copy of your workspace after deletion. We do not sell your data. We do not keep a backup of your account for later recovery.

Deletion takes effect when the request completes. There is no extra waiting period.

Delete some data without deleting the account

You can delete individual habits, tasks, and goals in the app without deleting your whole account.
